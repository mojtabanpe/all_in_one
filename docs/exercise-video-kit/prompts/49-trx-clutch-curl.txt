MAIN FIX
Keep both elbows approximately at shoulder height. Do not lower them beside the ribs when curling. Do not make a low row.

REFERENCE AND STYLE
Use approved AIO Coach v1 references. Preserve adult male coach, olive skin, short dark brown hair, clean-shaven face, teal T-shirt, charcoal joggers, white shoes with teal accents, identity and proportions. Polished stylized 3D animation. Light-gray studio, matte floor, soft lighting.

PREPARE A NEW STARTING IMAGE
Start in the CURLED position. Face the overhead anchor toward image LEFT. Lean slightly backward away from the anchor. Head, torso, pelvis, and legs aligned. Feet hip-width apart and planted.
Both elbows raised to shoulder height. Upper arms extend forward and outward. Both elbows bent. Handles close to upper chest. Elbows clearly separated from ribs. Shoulders down. Wrists straight.
Each hand firmly grips one handle. Fingers and thumbs wrapped around solid grips. Two continuous nonelastic straps connect handles to one fixed anchor. Both straps taut.
Fixed front-side three-quarter camera. Show both elbows clearly, whole body, handles, and strap paths.

ONE REPETITION — FIVE SECONDS
0.0–0.5: Hold curled pose. Both elbows at shoulder height.
0.5–2.0: Slowly straighten both elbows. Move hands away from chest toward anchor. Let aligned body lean farther backward from ankles. Keep upper arms raised. Do not lower elbows or move feet. Strap lengths constant.
2.0–2.5: Hold extended pose. Elbows softly extended without locking. Body leaning backward. Both hands gripping.
2.5–4.5: Bend elbows together. Bring handles toward upper chest. Draw aligned body slightly toward anchor. Keep elbows at shoulder height. Do not pull elbows down or sweep behind torso.
4.5–5.0: Hold exact curled opening pose.

ACCEPTANCE
Elbows approximately level with shoulders in every phase. Handles approach upper chest with raised elbows. Continuous grip, planted feet, aligned body. Straps taut and constant in length. Last frame matches first.
