ONE REPETITION — FIVE SECONDS
0.0–0.5 seconds: Hold the starting pose. Both hands forward. Straps taut. Feet fixed.
0.5–2.0 seconds: Lower the aligned body forward a small distance. Bend the RIGHT elbow. Bring the RIGHT handle toward the right side of the chest. Keep this elbow below shoulder height and moderately away from the ribs. Do not flare it straight sideways.
At the same time, open the LEFT arm toward the RIGHT edge of the image. Keep the LEFT elbow softly bent. Move the LEFT hand outward in a controlled arc at approximately chest height. Do not move that hand behind the torso. Keep gripping both handles.
Keep shoulders and pelvis facing forward together. Do not twist toward the opening arm. Do not move either foot.
2.0–2.5 seconds: Hold the asymmetric position. RIGHT elbow bent with handle near chest. LEFT arm open sideways with soft elbow. Both straps taut. Body aligned. Shoulders away from ears.
2.5–4.5 seconds: Press through the RIGHT handle. Gradually straighten the RIGHT elbow without locking it. Bring the LEFT arm forward along the same arc. Return both hands to their original shoulder-width positions. Return to the original slight forward lean. Feet fixed, straps taut.
4.5–5.0 seconds: Hold the original starting pose. Both hands forward again. Do not release handles, switch sides, or start another repetition.

Torso and pelvis remain aligned without twisting. Straps remain continuous, taut, and constant in length. Final pose matches the opening pose.
Avoid excessive shoulder range. Do not lock the sideways elbow. Do not shrug, sag at the hips, arch the lower back, or step forward.
