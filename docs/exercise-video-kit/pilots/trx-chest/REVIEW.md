# TRX chest press HyperFrames pilot

This eight-second motion study uses a custom vector figure in a fixed three-quarter view. The catalog reference
sets the opening and pressed poses. The two straps have fixed lengths, both feet are planted, and the opening
pose repeats at the end. The clip is intentionally silent, without titles or camera movement.

Render with `npx hyperframes@0.9.25 render -o trx-chest-hyperframes-pilot.mp4 --fps 30` from this directory.
HyperFrames 0.9.25 passed its checks. Five review frames cover framing and timing at full size and at
360 px. The rendered MP4 was verified as a silent H.264 stream at 1280 × 720, 240 frames at 30 fps,
and 582,246 bytes. The vector figure approximates the teal
shirt and charcoal joggers, and its face and proportions differ from the approved AIO Coach v1 reference.
In the opening pose, the projected near grip reads closer to the lower chest. The production version needs
a matching coach rig and a qualified form review, with attention to grip height and strap clearance.
